Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

About the application

GAZI PARK HH Backup is a private backup utility operated by the administrator of the GAZI PARK HH project. It is not offered as a public customer service.

Google Drive access

The utility uses Google OAuth authorization to create, access, restore, and manage its own backup files in the administrator's Google Drive account.

Data use and security

Google Drive access is used solely for backup and recovery. The backup system is designed to encrypt backup files locally before uploading them to Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the administrator's device. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

Data sharing

Backup files are stored using Google Drive. Google user data is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

The administrator controls backup retention. Backup files can be deleted from Google Drive and from local storage. Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
